# Easy Home Loans - Discovery Questionnaire

## 1. Company Basics

- What is the official brand name and spelling?
- Is the company already registered? If yes, under what legal name?
- Which city, state, or country does the business operate in?
- Who are the founders or key decision makers?
- Is this a new brand or an existing business going digital?

## 2. Business Model

- Are you a lender, loan advisor, DSA, broker, aggregator, or channel partner?
- Which banks, NBFCs, or financial institutions do you work with?
- How do you earn revenue: customer fee, lender commission, consulting fee, or another model?
- Do customers pay anything upfront?
- What is the average ticket size you want to target?
- Are there services you do not want to promote publicly?

## 3. Loan Products

- Which loan products do you currently offer?
- Do you offer new home loans?
- Do you offer home loan balance transfer?
- Do you offer top-up loans?
- Do you offer loan against property?
- Do you offer construction loans?
- Do you offer renovation or extension loans?
- Do you serve NRI customers?
- Do you handle commercial property loans?
- Which products are highest priority for launch?

## 4. Target Customers

- Who is the primary customer: salaried, self-employed, business owner, NRI, builder buyer, or existing loan borrower?
- Which income range do you mainly serve?
- Are customers mostly first-time buyers or existing loan customers?
- Do you target customers directly, through real estate agents, or through builders?
- What customer objections do you hear most often?

## 5. Customer Journey

- What happens after a customer submits an inquiry?
- Who calls the customer?
- How is eligibility checked?
- What documents are collected?
- How are bank options compared?
- Who fills and submits the loan application?
- Who tracks sanction, approval, and disbursement?
- What is the expected turnaround time?
- Where do most leads drop off?

## 6. Trust And Compliance

- Can we show partner bank or NBFC names/logos?
- Do you have testimonials or case studies?
- Do you have licenses, certifications, or official partner proof?
- Are there legal disclaimers we must include?
- Can we mention interest rates? If yes, who will update them?
- Can we claim "approval", "lowest rate", "zero fee", or "instant processing"?
- What customer data will be collected online?
- Do you already have a privacy policy and terms?

## 7. Digital Product Needs

- Do you need only a landing page or a full website?
- Do you need a loan eligibility form?
- Do you need EMI calculator?
- Do you need balance transfer savings calculator?
- Do you need document upload?
- Do you need WhatsApp integration?
- Do you need CRM or lead dashboard?
- Do you need admin users to manage leads?
- Do you need SMS, email, or call tracking?
- Do you need multilingual support?

## 8. Brand And Content

- Do you already have a logo?
- Do you have brand colors, fonts, or design references?
- Do you have photos, office images, founder images, or customer images?
- Do you have a tagline?
- Should the tone feel premium, friendly, local, corporate, or startup-like?
- Which competitor websites do you like?
- Which competitor websites do you dislike?

## 9. Launch Expectations

- What is the target launch date?
- What is the budget range?
- Who will approve design and content?
- Who will provide loan details and legal content?
- Is there an existing domain?
- Is there an existing hosting account?
- Will Alter Labs manage SEO, ads, and analytics after launch?

## 10. Decision Questions For The Call

- What is the single most important conversion action?
- What are the top three services to show first?
- What information must be visible on the homepage?
- What information should only be captured privately?
- What does success look like in the first 30 days after launch?
