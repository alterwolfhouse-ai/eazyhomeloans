# Easy Home Loans - Client Discussion Brief

## Current Understanding

Easy Home Loans is a new Alter Labs client project for a home-loan assistance
brand. The name suggests a service-led business that helps customers discover,
compare, apply for, transfer, or manage home loans with less friction.

This pass is for client discovery and proposal preparation only. We should not
commit to design, features, rates, claims, technical architecture, or delivery
scope until the client's real business process is understood.

## Likely Brand Promise

The strongest positioning is not just "we provide loans." It is:

"We make home loan decisions easier by helping customers choose the right loan,
understand eligibility, prepare documents, and complete the process with expert
support."

## Possible Core Services

- New home purchase loan
- Home construction loan
- Plot purchase plus construction loan
- Home loan balance transfer
- Top-up loan on existing home loan
- Home renovation or improvement loan
- Home extension loan
- Loan against property
- NRI home loan
- Joint home loan
- Pre-approved home loan
- Builder project loan support

## Customer Pain Points To Validate

- Customer does not know which bank or NBFC is best.
- Customer is confused about eligibility, EMI, documents, and approval chances.
- Customer wants a lower interest rate through balance transfer.
- Customer needs extra funds through a top-up loan.
- Customer has weak credit profile and needs guidance.
- Customer wants faster approval and less paperwork.
- Customer needs help coordinating with bank, builder, or property documents.

## Main Business Questions

- Are they a direct lender, broker, channel partner, DSA, financial advisor, or aggregator?
- Which banks or NBFCs do they work with?
- Do they charge customers, earn partner commission, or both?
- Which services are active today versus planned later?
- What locations do they serve?
- What makes them different from local loan agents and online marketplaces?
- What proof of trust can be shown publicly?

## Recommended First Client Call Flow

1. Business overview
2. Services currently offered
3. Customer types and geography
4. Loan process from lead to disbursement
5. Partner network and compliance constraints
6. Brand identity and content assets
7. Digital needs and conversion goals
8. Timeline, budget, and launch expectation
9. Next deliverables from Alter Labs

## Alter Labs Positioning In The Call

We should position Alter Labs as a product and growth partner, not only a website
vendor. The client needs clarity, trust, and conversion. The digital solution
should make the loan journey feel simple while capturing qualified leads for the
business team.

## Immediate Risk Areas

- Publicly showing interest rates without frequent updates.
- Claiming guaranteed approval.
- Using bank/NBFC logos without permission.
- Collecting sensitive documents without proper privacy, security, and consent.
- Showing "lowest rate" or "instant approval" without substantiation.
- Building too many features before confirming how the client's team actually works.
