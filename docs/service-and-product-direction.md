# Easy Home Loans - Service And Product Direction

## Service Taxonomy

### Primary Home Loan Services

1. New Home Loan
   - For customers buying a flat, house, or residential property.
   - Main user need: eligibility, EMI clarity, quick approval, bank comparison.

2. Home Loan Balance Transfer
   - For customers who already have a home loan and want to move it to another
     lender for better terms.
   - Main user need: savings calculation, lower EMI, lower rate, better tenure,
     top-up option.

3. Top-Up Loan
   - Additional loan amount on an existing home loan.
   - Main user need: extra funds without a completely new loan process.

4. Home Construction Loan
   - For customers who own land and want to build a house.
   - Main user need: staged disbursement, property document clarity, approval
     guidance.

5. Home Renovation Or Improvement Loan
   - For repair, interiors, painting, flooring, extension, or upgrades.
   - Main user need: quick funding and simple documentation.

6. Loan Against Property
   - For customers who mortgage an existing property to raise funds.
   - Main user need: higher loan amount, longer tenure, business or personal
     funding.

### Secondary Or Future Services

- NRI home loan
- Joint home loan
- Pre-approved home loan
- Commercial property loan
- Builder project loan assistance
- Credit score improvement guidance
- Property legal documentation support
- Insurance support linked to home loans

## Suggested Website Structure

### Homepage

Purpose: build trust quickly and capture qualified leads.

Key sections:

- Hero with direct value proposition
- Primary CTA: "Check Eligibility" or "Request Callback"
- Service cards for top loan products
- Why choose Easy Home Loans
- Simple process steps
- EMI or savings calculator preview
- Partner/trust area, only if verified
- Testimonials or success metrics, only if real
- FAQ
- Final CTA with phone and WhatsApp

### Services

Each service should have a dedicated page or section with:

- Who it is for
- Benefits
- Required documents
- Eligibility basics
- Process steps
- Common questions
- Lead form CTA

### Calculators

Recommended calculators:

- EMI calculator
- Eligibility estimate calculator
- Balance transfer savings calculator
- Top-up requirement calculator

Important: calculator outputs should be positioned as estimates, not final
approvals or financial advice.

### Lead Flow

Minimum lead form:

- Name
- Mobile number
- City
- Employment type
- Required loan type
- Approximate loan amount
- Existing loan yes/no
- Preferred callback time

Advanced lead form:

- Monthly income
- Existing EMI
- Property status
- Property value
- CIBIL score range
- Existing lender
- Current interest rate
- Outstanding loan amount
- Required top-up amount

## Recommended MVP Scope

For the first phase, keep the build conversion-focused:

- Responsive marketing website
- Service pages for 4 to 6 priority loan products
- EMI calculator
- Balance transfer savings calculator
- Lead capture form
- WhatsApp click-to-chat
- Admin-friendly lead delivery through email, CRM, or spreadsheet
- Basic analytics
- Privacy policy, terms, and disclaimers

## Possible Phase 2 Scope

- Customer document upload
- Lead dashboard or CRM
- Bank/NBFC matching logic
- Application status tracker
- Customer login
- Agent login
- Automated reminders
- SMS and email notifications
- Blog and SEO content system

## Content Principles

- Use simple language.
- Avoid promising guaranteed approval.
- Avoid publishing rates unless there is a process for updates.
- Explain benefits with examples, not exaggerated claims.
- Make phone and WhatsApp contact obvious.
- Build trust through process clarity, documents, and verified proof.

## Questions Before Design Starts

- Which 3 services are most profitable or important?
- What is the real customer process after inquiry?
- What proof of trust can we show?
- What is the primary contact action?
- Will the client provide content or should Alter Labs write it?
- Should the first version feel local and personal or corporate and scalable?
